1. System Architecture & Getting Started

TKanAI is an offline-capable, client-side agile management and AI engineering ecosystem. It requires zero cloud databases or API subscriptions—everything executes securely inside your web browser via Dexie.js (IndexedDB) with cross-tab real-time reactivity via the BroadcastChannel API.

How to Launch the System

  1. Open a terminal in the project directory: cd "c:\Users\ADMIN\Desktop\AI Ecosystem"
  2. Run the local development server: npm run dev
  3. Open your browser to: http://localhost:5173/
Database Cache & Name Updates If you modified team names in src/db/seedData.ts, your browser will still hold previous IndexedDB data. Go to System Settings in the sidebar and click Reset Data to refresh all records instantly.

Instant Role Switching

To simulate any role, click your profile avatar in the top-right header and select from the 10 personas. The UI views, action authorizations, and notifications adapt immediately without a logout.

2. End-to-End SDLC Project Workflow

Every project in TKanAI follows a structured 8-stage lifecycle. Each stage is driven by specific personas using specialized AI copilots.

1

Project Initiation & Scope

Roles: Org Admin (Vanuya), PM (Thivvikan)
Create project portfolio, define sprint cadences, and assemble cross-functional rosters.

2

Requirements & Ambiguity Intelligence

Roles: BA (Kanushi), PO (Ushamini), Client (Sutharsini)
Draft FR/NFRs, execute AI Ambiguity Scoring, and conduct formal approvals.

3

Backlog Breakdown & User Stories

Roles: PO (Ushamini), BA (Kanushi)
Use the AI Story Generator to convert requirements into user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria.

4

Sprint Planning & Kanban Execution

Roles: PM (Thivvikan), Dev (Kajakaran), Designer (Sarangn)
Commit stories to sprints, auto-decompose into subtasks, and advance cards through Kanban swimlanes.

5

QA Testing & AI Bug Lab

Roles: QA Lead (Mayoory), Dev (Kajakaran)
Author test suites, record execution results, and trigger AI Root Cause Analysis (RCA) on defects.

6

Meetings & AI Decision Extractor

Roles: All Team Members
Log sprint ceremonies and use AI to extract Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) and action items.

7

Client Portal & Change Requests (CR)

Roles: Client (Sutharsini), PM (Thivvikan)
Submit scope change requests and run AI Impact Analysis estimating schedule, cost, and test risks.

8

Risk Radar & Executive Delivery

Roles: PM (Thivvikan), Stakeholder (Hariarthan)
Monitor the AI Risk Radar probability matrix and generate printable executive reports.

Living Traceability Guarantee Every artifact connects end-to-end: Requirement ➔ User Story ➔ Task ➔ QA Test Case ➔ Defect. Open the Traceability Matrix view at any time to verify 100% test coverage and ensure zero orphan requirements.

3. Role-by-Role Step-by-Step Practical Guides

1. Organization Administrator Persona: Vanuya
Primary Views: Org Dashboard, Project Portfolio, System Settings, Global Search

Mission: Oversee enterprise health, instantiate projects, configure governance parameters, and maintain database integrity.

  1. Step 1 — Switch Role: Click the top-right avatar and switch to Vanuya (Org Admin).
  2. Step 2 — Inspect Org Health: Go to Organization Dashboard to view macro KPIs: total projects, completed tasks, open critical bugs, and overall document quality score.
  3. Step 3 — Create a New Project: Open Project Portfolio, click "Create Project", specify project key, title, deadline, and assigned leads.
  4. Step 4 — Database Backup: Navigate to System Settings and click "Export Database" to save a complete JSON backup of the organization state.
2. Project Manager (PM) Persona: Thivvikan
Primary Views: Kanban Board, Sprint Analytics, AI Risk Radar, Meetings, Change Requests

Mission: Manage sprint cadences, balance team workloads, evaluate client change requests, and mitigate project delivery risks.

  1. Step 1 — Monitor Active Sprint: Open Sprint Analytics to review the burndown velocity chart, remaining story points, and sprint deadline.
  2. Step 2 — Orchestrate the Kanban Board: Go to Kanban Board. Drag cards between swimlanes (To Do ➔ In Progress ➔ Code Review ➔ QA Ready ➔ Done). PMs have unrestricted rights to reassign or move any card.
  3. Step 3 — Evaluate Client Change Requests: When the Client submits a Change Request (CR-001), open the CR detail view, review the AI Impact Analysis (cost, delay, affected tasks), and formally approve or reject with decision notes.
  4. Step 4 — Review the AI Risk Radar: Open AI Risk Radar to review high-impact risks (e.g., concurrency race conditions, developer overallocation) and assign mitigation tasks.
3. Product Owner (PO) Persona: Ushamini
Primary Views: Requirements & AI, Backlog & Stories, Documentation Hub

Mission: Define product vision, prioritize sprint backlogs, approve requirement specifications, and validate deliverable scope.

  1. Step 1 — Review & Approve Requirements: Open Requirements & AI. Review requirements drafted by the BA. Click "Approve Requirement" to lock the scope for engineering.
  2. Step 2 — Prioritize the Product Backlog: Open Backlog & Stories. Drag and reorder user stories to define sprint priorities. Ensure all high-priority stories have estimated story points.
  3. Step 3 — Generate User Stories via AI: In the Requirements view, select a requirement and click "AI Story Generator" to convert technical clauses into standard user stories with acceptance criteria.
  4. Step 4 — Approve Living Specifications: Navigate to Documentation Hub, review SRS v1.2, and provide formal Product Owner sign-off.
4. Business Analyst (BA) Persona: Kanushi
Primary Views: Requirements & AI, Traceability Matrix, Documentation Hub

Mission: Gather functional/non-functional requirements, eliminate specification ambiguity, and link user stories to business goals.

  1. Step 1 — Author Requirements: Open Requirements & AI and click "Create Requirement". Specify category (FR/NFR/Security), priority, description, and acceptance criteria.
  2. Step 2 — Run AI Ambiguity Analyzer: Select any requirement and click "Analyze Ambiguity". The AI inspects for vague terms (e.g., "fast", "user-friendly") and provides actionable, measurable rewrites with a confidence score.
  3. Step 3 — Inspect Traceability: Open Traceability Matrix to check that every requirement is linked downstream to at least one User Story and QA Test Case.
  4. Step 4 — Maintain Documentation: Use Documentation Hub to update the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) in markdown format.
5. Senior Developer Persona: Kajakaran
Primary Views: Kanban Board, Issues & AI Bug Lab, Documentation Hub

Mission: Implement features, resolve defects, conduct code reviews, and author architecture decision records.

  1. Step 1 — Check Assigned Tasks: Open Kanban Board and locate tasks assigned to you. Drag your active task from To Do to In Progress.
  2. Step 2 — Decompose Stories into Tasks: In Backlog & Stories, use the AI Task Decomposer to split user stories into backend, frontend, and database subtasks.
  3. Step 3 — Resolve Bugs with AI Root Cause Analysis: Open Issues & AI Bug Lab. Select an assigned bug (e.g., BUG-001). Click "AI Root Cause Analysis" to view code fix recommendations (e.g., Redis distributed lock with Lua script). Implement the fix and mark the bug as Resolved.
  4. Step 4 — Advance to Code Review: Move your completed task card to the Code Review column on the Kanban board.
6. QA Engineer Persona: Mayoory
Primary Views: QA Test Suites, Issues & AI Bug Lab, Traceability Matrix

Mission: Author test cases, execute manual/automated regression runs, report bugs, and perform final defect closure.

  1. Step 1 — Execute Test Suites: Open QA Test Suites. Select a suite (e.g., Concurrency & Checkout Suite). Execute test cases and record results: Passed, Failed, or Blocked.
  2. Step 2 — Auto-File Bug on Test Failure: When a test case fails, click "Log Bug from Failure". The system automatically populates a defect ticket linked directly to the failed test case.
  3. Step 3 — Verify & Close Resolved Bugs: In Issues & AI Bug Lab, filter for bugs in Resolved status. Re-test the fix. If successful, perform the QA sign-off to mark the bug as Closed.
  4. Step 4 — Kanban QA Sign-off: Move verified feature cards from QA Ready to Done on the Kanban board.
7. UI/UX Designer Persona: Sarangn
Primary Views: Documentation Hub, Kanban Board, Issues & AI Bug Lab

Mission: Design accessible interfaces, maintain design tokens, and review UI implementations against wireframes.

  1. Step 1 — Document Design Guidelines: In Documentation Hub, author and update Design System Specs, interactive wireframe mockups, and mobile responsive guidelines.
  2. Step 2 — Track Design Tasks: Move design tasks (e.g., "Interactive 2D Bus Seat Selector") on the Kanban Board to keep engineering aligned.
  3. Step 3 — File UI/UX & Accessibility Defects: Open Issues & AI Bug Lab to report visual layout bugs, contrast issues, or touch-target defects.
8. DevOps Engineer Persona: Karikaalan
Primary Views: Kanban Board, AI Risk Radar, Documentation Hub

Mission: Ensure pipeline reliability, infrastructure scalability, database indexing, and automated deployment stability.

  1. Step 1 — Infrastructure Execution: On the Kanban Board, track and complete infrastructure tasks (e.g., Redis cluster configuration, database query indexing).
  2. Step 2 — Maintain Runbooks: Author deployment runbooks and rollback procedures in the Documentation Hub.
  3. Step 3 — Support Risk Mitigations: Review technical items on the AI Risk Radar to assist developers in resolving server-side bottlenecks.
9. Client / Sponsor Persona: Sutharsini
Primary Views: Client Portal, Requirements & AI, Executive Reports

Mission: Monitor milestone progress, approve project deliverables, and submit change requests (CR) with transparent impact modeling.

  1. Step 1 — Access the Client Portal: Click Client Portal in the sidebar to review overall project delivery progress and active milestones.
  2. Step 2 — Submit a Change Request: Click "Submit Change Request" (e.g., Requesting a 2-Hour Ticket Cancellation Rule). State the business justification and desired deadline.
  3. Step 3 — Review AI Impact Analysis: View the automatically generated impact breakdown: affected requirements, development hours, and estimated cost adjustments.
  4. Step 4 — Milestone Sign-off: Formally accept delivery milestones once demonstrated during sprint reviews.
10. Executive Stakeholder Persona: Hariarthan
Primary Views: Org Dashboard, Sprint Analytics, AI Risk Radar, Executive Reports

Mission: High-level organizational governance, sprint velocity oversight, financial ROI tracking, and executive sign-off.

  1. Step 1 — Review High-Level Dashboards: Open Organization Dashboard for enterprise-level delivery metrics and team workloads.
  2. Step 2 — Track Velocity Trends: Open Sprint Analytics to inspect velocity across historical sprints.
  3. Step 3 — Inspect Critical Risks: Open AI Risk Radar to ensure high-severity risks have active mitigation strategies.
  4. Step 4 — Generate Executive Reports: Navigate to Executive Reports to preview and print clean PDF summaries.

4. TKanAI Copilot & Power Features

Interactive AI Assistant Drawer

Click the ✨ AI Assistant icon in the top header on any page to open the TKanAI Project Copilot. The copilot possesses real-time awareness of all IndexedDB entities.

User Prompt / Query Copilot Action & Intelligence
"What is the current project status?" Summarizes active sprint burndown, total story points, open defects, and overall schedule compliance.
"Which tasks are delayed or blocked?" Scans Kanban tasks, filters for blocker flags or approaching deadlines, and alerts assignees.
"What is our highest risk item?" Retrieves the highest-scoring risk from the Risk Radar and recites the active mitigation plan.
"How is developer workload distributed?" Analyzes active task count and remaining hours per engineer to prevent burnout.

Universal Spotlight Search (Ctrl+K)

Press Ctrl + K (or Cmd + K on macOS) to invoke universal spotlight search. Jump instantly to any task (TASK-101), requirement (REQ-001), bug (BUG-001), or document.

6. Quick Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: Why does my browser still show old team member names?
A: IndexedDB caches data after the initial load. Go to System Settings in the sidebar, click Reset Data, and confirm. All updated names will appear immediately.

Q: Can multiple team members test this together?
A: Yes! Open two browser tabs side-by-side (e.g., Tab 1 as Developer Kajakaran and Tab 2 as QA Mayoory). When Kajakaran moves a card or resolves a bug in Tab 1, Mayoory's tab in Tab 2 updates in real time via BroadcastChannel.

Q: How do I export or backup my work?
A: In System Settings, click Export Database to save a complete JSON archive of all projects, requirements, tasks, bugs, and meetings.
